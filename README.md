# OSOWA

**The open operating system for companies.**

OSOWA is an open source business operating system that lets any company in the world run its entire business from one place: CRM, conversations, marketing, websites, reputation, analytics, automations and AI agents, assembled per sector into one personalised environment.

Initiated by [Ocean Wave Agency](https://oceanwaveagency.com) in Antwerp, Belgium.

## Mission

Help companies anywhere in the world grow in a sustainable way.

Most companies, from a one-person business to a group with many locations, run on a patchwork of disconnected tools. That costs money, time and control over their own data, and it is out of reach for many companies in large parts of the world. OSOWA replaces that patchwork with one open, privacy-first operating system that adapts itself to the sector of the company.

## How it works

1. A company signs up and selects its sector.
2. OSOWA proposes the modules that sector actually needs. The company activates only those.
3. A Digital and Legal Audit of the company's current online presence (website, visibility, GDPR, cookies, legal pages) is generated inside the platform and turned into a concrete action plan.
4. The company runs its daily operations from one dashboard, with AI agents and automations doing the repetitive work.

Everything is self-service. There is no sales call and no onboarding project: the software explains itself.

## Modules

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| Home | One canvas with the single most important number and what needs attention today |
| Audit | Automated Digital and Legal Audit with action plan |
| CRM | Contacts, companies, pipeline, tasks |
| Conversations | Unified inbox: email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, web chat, forms |
| Marketing | Social planner, campaigns, paid ads, email campaigns |
| Websites | Pages, blocks editor, SEO, domains |
| Reputation | Reviews and reputation management |
| Analytics | Reach, conversion, revenue per channel |
| Automations | Workflow builder and triggers |
| AI | In-app assistant and AI agents per module |
| Privacy | GDPR tooling: access log, retention periods, export, right to erasure |
| Billing | Subscriptions, invoices, usage |
| 3D Tours | Walkable 3D environments from photos and floor plans |

See [docs/MODULES.md](docs/MODULES.md) for the module specification and [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) for the technical structure.

## Status

OSOWA is in early rollout with its first paying customers in Belgium. The production platform currently runs at os.oceanwaveagency.com. The codebase is being prepared for public release module by module. Follow [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for progress.

## Principles

- **Open**: free to use, extend and self-host.
- **Privacy-first**: built for GDPR from day one, no medical or patient data.
- **Sector-aware**: modules and defaults per sector, contributed by the community.
- **Self-service**: the software explains itself, no consultant required.
- **Sustainable**: a company pays only for what it uses and keeps control of its own data.
